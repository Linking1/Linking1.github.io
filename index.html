Wenbin Zhu
Assistant Researcher, Nankai University

He received the B.E. degree from Nankai University in 2019 and the Ph.D. degree from Nankai University in 2025. He is now an assistant researcher in the Department of Automation and Intelligent Science, College of AI, Nankai University, and a member of the Laboratory for Advanced Perception and Control (LAPC). His research interests include mobile robot SLAM and object detection. He has authored or co-authored papers in journals and conferences such as IEEE T-RO, TSMC, T-IM, PR, NeurIPS, ICPR and ICIVC. He was a recipient of the 3rd-place award on the Tianjin "Haihe Talents" postdoctoral innovation and entrepreneurship contest.
